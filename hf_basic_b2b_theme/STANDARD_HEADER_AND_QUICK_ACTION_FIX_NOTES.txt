HisabFlow Basic B2B Theme - v19.0.1.3.1
=========================================

1. Homepage Search Separation
-----------------------------
- The custom "Search anything" control is rendered only while the HisabFlow
  homepage application is active.
- Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Accounting, Apps and all other Odoo screens use
  only their own native control-panel search, so duplicate search bars are gone.
- Ctrl+K remains the homepage search interaction.

2. Company Switching
--------------------
- The native Odoo company selector remains visible and clickable.
- Dashboard figures continue to use env.company and therefore refresh for the
  company selected through Odoo's standard company switcher.
- Dashboard record drill-down domains are generated for the active company.

3. Quick Actions
----------------
- Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Accounting, Manufacturing and Point of Sale no
  longer try to open root app menus that may not contain an action.
- Every Overview Quick Action now opens an explicit ir.actions.act_window with
  the correct model, current-company domain and selected date period.
- KPI cards and Business Snapshot rows open their own filtered records.
- Recent Business Activity rows open the selected record, while View All opens
  the relevant invoices/vendor-bills list for the chosen date range.
- Client-side navigation includes access-error handling and a visible Odoo
  notification instead of an unhandled UI error.

4. Layout
---------
- The HisabFlow left sidebar is retained on desktop.
- The sidebar remains collapsible and remembers its state.
- On smaller screens, the custom sidebar is hidden and Odoo's native app menu
  receives the full viewport.
