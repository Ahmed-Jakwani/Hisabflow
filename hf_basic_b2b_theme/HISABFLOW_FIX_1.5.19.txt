HisabFlow Theme 19.0.1.5.19
============================

Dashboard search — placed right after Overview:

Client feedback: the true-centered search box from v1.5.18 floats in the
middle of the header, but the requested layout is simply

    Overview   [ Search anything... ]                    [native systray]

with the search box immediately to the right of "Overview" (not centered
in the header).

Fix
---
- `.o_menu_sections` (native "Overview" label) keeps its own natural
  width instead of being stretched to an equal flex share.
- `.hf-dashboard-global-search` follows it directly in normal document
  flow with a small 22px gap - no `position: fixed/absolute`, no `vw`
  math.
- `.o_menu_systray` keeps `margin-left: auto`, so the native systray icons
  stay pinned to the far right of the header, same as before.

Only the >= 1200px desktop dashboard header is affected. Other apps and
smaller breakpoints are untouched.
